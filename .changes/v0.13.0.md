## [0.13.0] - 2026-11-15

### Added

- Releases now publish the Explorer. The `ghcr.io/zcashfoundation/zinder-explorer` image can replace the locally built Explorer in the Compose overlay when it is pulled and started without a build, and the GNU/Linux binary archives include the `zinder-explorer` executable. For hosts that attach one volume to one container, such as Railway, the new `ghcr.io/zcashfoundation/zinder-wallet-serving-explorer` image runs wallet serving with Explorer views enabled on ingest. It serves `ExplorerQuery` on port 9068 and Explorer operations on port 9069, and starts the Explorer once wallet serving answers. If the Explorer exits, only the Explorer restarts, and wallet serving continues. The image healthcheck covers wallet serving alone, so monitor Explorer readiness on port 9069. `ZINDER_EXPLORER__*` variables reach only the Explorer. A volume built by `zinder-wallet-serving` can switch to this image: ingest then builds the Explorer views from canonical history. See the wallet-serving deployment runbook. ([#165](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/165))

### Fixed

- `zinder-projector` no longer exits with `canonical secondary did not reach the writer fence before the convergence deadline` when a burst of blocks keeps the canonical writer committing ahead of it, which restarted every process in the single-volume wallet-serving runtime. It keeps applying the blocks its canonical secondary already holds while the writer is ahead, including right after a restart, each still authenticated against the writer's retained events, and when an attempt to reach the writer lasts `projector.fence_convergence_timeout_seconds` it logs `canonical_secondary_behind_writer`, reports `replica_lagging`, renews its canonical retention lease, and tries again. A ready projector also stays `ready` while its wallet trails the writer by any number of blocks, as long as it applies a block at least every 30 seconds and the trail lasts less than 2 minutes; it previously reported `syncing` whenever it trailed by two or more blocks. ([#192](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/192))
- Slow or abandoned `TransparentAddressUnspentOutputs` reads no longer stop `zinder-query` or `zinder-compat-lightwalletd` from publishing new chain epochs for every client. The read now sends outputs as it reads them, 1,024 at a time, reads storage only as fast as the client consumes, and stops when the client cancels or disconnects; the first outputs of a large address arrive after one page instead of after the whole set. A request may keep reading a serving pair for at most 30 seconds after a newer one replaces it and leaves retention. An unspent-output or full-block stream still reading past that limit ends with `FAILED_PRECONDITION` and `CHAIN_EPOCH_PIN_UNAVAILABLE`, which clients already treat as a signal to restart at a newer epoch. `zinder_wallet_serving_pair_publisher_revoked_pairs_total` and the `wallet_serving_pair_reads_revoked` warning report each such revocation. ([#193](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/193))
- Explorer view replay that resumes partway through history, as after a restart or upgrade, now warms its prevout cache from canonical history before it continues, instead of resolving most transparent spends through one canonical read at a time, and the canonical reads that remain run on up to 16 threads; the transparent-address ranking build does the same, and both log `materialized_view_prevout_cache_warmed`. On a store whose Explorer views were built without the transparent-address ranking, ingest now builds the ranking beside the view replay and joins it at the height the other views have reached, so `explorer.transparent_address.activity_v2` and `explorer.transparent_address.ranking_v1` are advertised long before replay reaches the tip; ingest logs `transparent_address_ranking_joined_replay`, and `zinder_materialized_view_transparent_address_ranking_build_height` and `zinder_materialized_view_transparent_address_ranking_build_target_height` report the build's progress. `zinder_materialized_view_prevout_cache_outputs{cache}` and `zinder_materialized_view_prevout_cache_evicted_outputs_total{cache}` report prevout cache use. Explorer view files now compress with ZSTD, which used about a third less disk than the previous Snappy default on a transparent-dense fixture; existing view stores open in place and shrink as compaction rewrites their files. ([#194](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/194))
