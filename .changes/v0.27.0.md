## [0.27.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added

- Exported signed-catalog read telemetry (`zinder_compat_published_history_read_bytes_total` pre-registered at zero and `zinder_compat_published_history_read_duration_seconds`, both labelled by `slab` `shielded` and `nullifier`) plus rate-limited cgroup v2 memory-pressure diagnostics on `wait_timeout` refusals, and documented cgroup v2 page-cache troubleshooting in `docs/reference/metrics.md`. ([#618](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/618))
- Exported live `EncodedTail` cache telemetry (`zinder_compat_encoded_tail_lookups_total`, `zinder_compat_encoded_tail_fills_total`, `zinder_compat_encoded_tail_byte_capped_fills_total`, `zinder_compat_encoded_tail_byte_cap_evictions_total`, and `zinder_compat_encoded_tail_bytes`), documented in `docs/reference/metrics.md`. ([#655](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/655))

### Changed

- Raised the default canonical-store RocksDB reader open-file limit (`storage.canonical.rocksdb.max_open_files`) to `1024`–`2048` based on detected container memory (`2048` without a limit), configured secondary RocksDB table caches with 4 shards (`table_cache_numshardbits = 2`) to prevent per-shard table-reader eviction on Mainnet and Testnet stores, and set `65,536` soft and hard `nofile` limits across all Zinder store-opening Compose services. ([#615](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/615))
- Reduced per-request CPU on small unary gRPC responses (`<= 4 KiB`, such as `GetTreeState`, `GetLatestTreeState`, `GetLatestBlock`, and `GetLightdInfo`) by probing the frame whole inside the HTTP/2 release gate instead of splitting off its trailing byte into a second DATA frame, preserving all send-stall reset and shutdown termination guarantees. ([#617](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/617))
- Cached the visible-tip commitment-tree checkpoint per wallet-serving generation and served pre-encoded hexadecimal tree states without epoch-pin retention on wallet-mode `GetLatestTreeState` and `GetTreeState` height lookups, eliminating per-call RocksDB reads and hexadecimal re-encoding on warm requests while preserving reorg and history-floor guarantees. ([#629](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/629))
- Serve non-transparent lightwalletd `GetBlockRange` and `GetBlockRangeNullifiers` `pool_types` subsets (such as `[PoolType::Orchard]`) from published `ZSHRNG01` segments and the in-memory shielded tail instead of falling back to canonical RocksDB reads. ([#630](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/630))
- Batched canonical transaction-location, retained-blob, and block-header multi-get lookups and pipelined up to 8 concurrent `ForwardedReadKind::History` cold raw-transaction fetches per stream in wallet-mode `GetTaddressTxids` and `GetTaddressTransactions`, preserving canonical `(block_height, tx_index)` wire order, SHA-256 digest verification, `GetTransaction` slot reservations, and immediate cancellation on client disconnect or serving-pair revocation. ([#632](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/632))
- Batch `ZSHRNG01` sequential short-frame slab reads up to 512 KiB and coalesce uncompressed lightwalletd `GetBlockRange` and `GetBlockRangeNullifiers` streams into 8 KiB opening and 32 KiB subsequent gRPC batches to improve high-concurrency range throughput on multi-client readers. ([#648](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/648))
- Defaulted the single-host Compose deployment (`deploy/docker-compose.yml`) to `ZINDER_DEPLOYMENT__PROFILE=wallet` (`raw_blob_policy = "none"`, with `wallet-archive` override, `!reset null` profile clearing in `deploy/docker-compose.explorer.yml`, and `deploy/docker-compose.transactions.yml` for upgrading pre-profile `transactions` stores in place), forwarded `ZINDER_INGEST__SOURCE` to `zinder-ingest`, bounded `zinder-history-publisher` with a 512 MiB `/tmp` tmpfs mount and `--workers "${ZINDER_HISTORY_PUBLISHER_WORKERS:-1}"`, added a turn-key projector-free `deploy/docker-compose.light-wallet.yml` bundle with matching two-target Prometheus scrape/expected rules, and updated `deploy/systemd/zinder.service` to pass `--no-build` without a destructive `ExecStartPre` teardown. ([#650](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/650))
- Cache unchanged shielded commitment-tree checkpoints across worker threads and pipeline 64-block commitment-tree batches ahead of canonical RocksDB SST staging during fresh store construction when `ingest.construction.tree_hashing_workers` exceeds one. ([#651](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/651))
- Enabled `--features pasta_curves/asm` by default (`ZINDER_PASTA_ASM=1`) across `deploy/Dockerfile`, release binary archives, container images, and Compose definitions so x86-64 builds select the `BMI2 + ADX` assembly backend (with startup verification, explicit `required_cpu_features` metadata `["adx","pclmulqdq"]` validated in release archive `BUILD-INFO.json`, and `ZINDER_PASTA_ASM=0` fallback for pre-Broadwell/Haswell hosts) and AArch64 builds select ARMv8-A assembly. ([#659](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/659))

### Fixed

- Eliminated uncached per-epoch disk reads during canonical secondary open and catch-up by scanning and caching retained `chain_epoch` and `chain_event` rows with 10-bits-per-key Bloom filters, preventing multi-second secondary refresh stalls and `SERVED_TIP_MISMATCH` errors under `O_DIRECT`. ([#628](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/628))
- Raised forwarded-read arrival waiter queues (`forwarded_read_waiters_per_slot` default from `16` to `64`, accepted range `1..=256`, and unspent-output response waiting places from `128` to `1,024`) and released streamed unspent-output response permits on yielding the final row so concurrent `light-wallet` `GetAddressUtxosStream` bursts queue and drain instead of returning `UNAVAILABLE`. ([#631](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/631))
- Hardened the local observability Compose stack to bind Prometheus and Grafana to `127.0.0.1` by default without `--web.enable-admin-api`, fixed RocksDB property unit and `store_role` rendering plus `zinder-query` availability in the overview Grafana dashboard, added dashboard panels and Prometheus alert rules for v0.26.0 exported signals, and updated `scripts/observability-smoke.sh` to report unexercised backup/restore as `not_exercised` instead of `blocked`. ([#647](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/647))
- Default single-volume `wallet-serving` containers to `ZINDER_DEPLOYMENT__PROFILE=wallet` when Explorer views and `ZINDER_STORAGE__RAW_BLOB_POLICY` are unset (supporting `wallet-archive` and legacy profile-free `ZINDER_STORAGE__RAW_BLOB_POLICY=transactions` upgrades while rejecting `light-wallet` and Explorer-view profile conflicts), switch packaged `wallet-serving` ingest to `zebra-indexer-grpc` without a hardcoded `raw_blob_policy`, align `zinder-explorer` default `storage.secondary_path` to `/var/lib/zinder/explorer/secondary`, update the `zinder-ingest` image `HEALTHCHECK` to probe `/readyz`, and update ADR-0023, the configuration reference, and the single-volume runbook. ([#652](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/652))
- Released upstream `zinder-ingest` mempool event subscriptions immediately when a lightwalletd `GetMempoolStream` call closes on a chain tip change or client disconnect, even when outbound stream delivery is backpressured or the mempool is idle. ([#653](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/653))
- Bounded mempool snapshot pages to 3.5 MiB of encoded protobuf alongside `max_entries`, pinned multi-page snapshot walks to point-in-time mempool membership, and drained continuation pages across `ExplorerQuery` so `WalletQuery.MempoolSnapshot`, `ExplorerQuery` mempool/overview handlers, `GetMempoolTx`, and `GetMempoolStream` serve coherent snapshots past the 4 MiB default gRPC decode limit while keeping `zinder-query` health probes ready and surfacing oversized single-entry refusals with typed `UNSUPPORTED_WALLET_ENCODING` `ErrorInfo`. ([#654](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/654))
