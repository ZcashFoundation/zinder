## [0.12.0] - 2026-11-14

### Fixed

- `zinder-projector` no longer reports `syncing` with `lag_blocks: 1` for a moment after every block: a ready projector within one block of the canonical tip stays `ready` while it applies the block, and reports `syncing` only if 30 seconds pass without it applying a block or it trails by more than one block. `zinder-query` now exits promptly on `SIGTERM` even while wallets hold `ChainEvents` or `MempoolEvents` streams open: it ends every open stream with `UNAVAILABLE` and readiness cause `shutting_down`, and stops waiting for in-flight calls after 10 seconds, so it stops within the wallet-serving supervisor's 20-second grace window. ([#182](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/182))
- `ExplorerQuery.TransparentAddressActivity` no longer fails with `FAILED_PRECONDITION` while the transparent-address ranking is ahead of the requested chain epoch, which happened for most of every block interval. Address summaries, ranking coverage, and activity rows now describe the epoch's visible tip. A read still fails with `FAILED_PRECONDITION` when the ranking holds a different block than the epoch, including while it trails the epoch on a replaced branch, or when it would need to rewind more than 1,000 blocks. The Cipherscan adapter no longer marks `/api/address/:address` degraded only because the Explorer views lead the pinned epoch; it does so when ranking coverage stops short of the epoch's tip. ([#183](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/183))
- Explorer view replay and the transparent-address ranking build no longer re-read the producing block for each transparent spend of an output they already replayed, which made transparent-heavy history such as early Mainnet replay at a few hundred blocks per minute; they keep up to about 375 MB of unspent outputs in memory while they walk history and release it at the tip or under memory pressure. Explorer view replay, the ranking build, and historical view backfills now also pause while the volume holding `storage.path` has less available space than the new `ingest.explorer_views_min_free_bytes` (`ZINDER_INGEST__EXPLORER_VIEWS_MIN_FREE_BYTES`, default 10 GiB, `0` disables), logging `materialized_view_replay_volume_space_low`, and resume on their own once a tenth more is available; canonical ingest and wallet serving keep running. Existing Explorer view stores resume in place. ([#184](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zinder/pull/184))
